How to play

Can you spot a phishing email?

You work for SuperDevs. Your company uses @superdevs.example email addresses. Check the spelling carefully—but remember that a matching domain alone does not prove an email is safe.

  1. Read each email. Check the sender, message, and links.
  2. Choose Report Phishing if it looks suspicious, or Trust Email if it looks safe.
  3. When a quiz appears, select all the correct answers and submit.

Correct decision: +10 points

Reported a safe email: 0 points

Trusted a phishing email: 0 points

Review all 10 emails and finish the quizzes to complete the game.
